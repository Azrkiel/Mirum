## Remaining risks and blockers

No B01 blocker. The unmodified build failed because Avast intercepts PyPI TLS;
the documented local trusted-root build passed without disabling verification.
No host certificate or dependency change is committed. Existing Pydantic and
pyparsing deprecation warnings remain outside this task.

Native stage defaults are 30 s, 1,536 MiB address space and 1 MiB captured output.
The small reference workload is comfortably below the proposed 420 s V1 budget
and its files below 32 MiB. Worst-case four-candidate runtime, concurrent legacy
workloads, V1 publication/storage/deadline/cancellation enforcement and complete
release checks remain for their owning tasks. This is not a worst-case proof.
