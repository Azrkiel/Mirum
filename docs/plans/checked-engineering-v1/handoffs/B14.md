## Limitations

1. Manual `RELEASE.md` blocks (`save-rollback-images`, `rollback`, `enable-ai`, `disable-ai`, `uninstall`, `remove-volume`) are intentionally not executed in CI (`run_release_md.py` never extracts them). The rollback sequence is reviewed, not exercised; restart-replacement on the same volume is exercised (S12, release-docs). Closing it: a CI job that builds two tags, upgrades and rolls back with a volume, then compares retained fingerprints.
2. S08c is recorded `not_injected` (the kernel refused the read-only remount with EBUSY); observation only. Closing it: a blockdev helper or loopback device on the runner.
3. The ZIP identifies the reviewed brief (revision, policy versions) and every payload by hash, but does not embed the software commit or image ID; those are in the evidence JSON. Replay needs only the brief and the pinned image.
4. Time-based expiry runs are accelerated (A), as labelled in QUALIFICATION; the six-hour real expiry was not waited out.
5. Live rows cite B13 run 5 and step 12 (earlier SHAs `f11cf90`, `86e6326`); the freeze made no live call. The bridge is the parity tests. If AI transport or configuration changes, qualification is stale and returns to B13.
6. Stale wording left alone (code is out of scope): comments in `backend/config.py:80` and `docker-compose.yml:21` still say AI drafts are "not live-qualified"; `RELEASE.md` "Scoped AI setup" says final qualification "still depends on the release evidence gates" (now satisfied by step 13); `README.md` still says the glider "PicoGK manufacturing builder" is unqualified, which concerns the legacy glider CEM, not the regen chamber.
