## Remaining risks and blockers

No B03 blocker. Hashes are consistency identities, not external authenticity,
physical evidence or authentication of the reviewer. Consumers must validate
deserialized models and never use Pydantic's validation-bypass helpers with
untrusted data. Unknown capability/policy versions require preparation/review
again; there is no implicit upgrade.

B04 must translate real worker outputs; B08 must assess actual methods/limits;
B10 must hash delivered bytes and measure stored size. A typed evidence claim
does not establish those facts on its own. B05 owns persistence/revision conflict
handling and clarification flows; concurrency, browser controls and delivery
mutation checks remain their full V1 gates. Existing dependency warnings and
the B01 host TLS trust prerequisite remain documented, not task blockers.
