### P6 E03 preparation (Claude worker, docs only; parallel with wave 2)

Recommended family (lead, under the owner's Q3 delegation): **a rectangular flat plate in bending under
uniform pressure, clamped or simply supported edges, isotropic linear-elastic.** Why it fits a combined
probabilistic + deterministic generator:
- AI proposes typed inputs (span, aspect ratio, pressure, edge condition, material); the deterministic
  side sizes thickness and checks stress and deflection, the same reviewed-brief boundary as the beam.
- Independent deterministic benchmarks exist: Navier/Levy series and Timoshenko tables. The solver choice
  (closed form or 2-D Kirchhoff FE) can be cross-checked against them, so beam evidence is never reused.
- It is a different structural model (2-D bending) with a small, clear exclusion list (no holes,
  stiffeners, large deflection, buckling, composites).
Alternatives to record: L-bracket (3-D FEA with singular corners; card step 4 cost) and an Euler column
(buckling knockdown is empirical). Output: `proposals/E03-plate-bending.md` + a blocked `handoffs/E03.md`
naming the decision needed (owner or domain-engineer sign-off of the written contract before any solver).
